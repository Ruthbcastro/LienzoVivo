## Use Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Residential Art Buyer**  
Mariana, 32, wants to decorate her new apartment's living room with artwork that matches her modern style. She uses the app to explore pieces by size and style, and the AR feature helps her visualize how the artworks would look in her space, making it easier to compare options and choose the best fit. Her main need is to ensure the artwork complements her home and that the purchase is easy and secure. Her behavior includes exploring different options, evaluating how each piece fits in her décor, and making informed purchasing decisions.

**Scenario 2: Art Collector**  
Juan, 45, is looking to expand his personal collection with works from emerging artists. He browses online galleries and uses the AR feature to see how new pieces would integrate with his existing collection and workspace. This helps him assess scale and visual harmony before buying. His need is to validate authenticity, quality, and compatibility with his collection. His behavior includes researching artists, comparing works, and deciding based on detailed information and visual assessment.

**Scenario 3: Decoration Enthusiast / Inspirational User**  
Laura, 27, wants ideas to redecorate her room and explore new art trends. She explores different styles and colors in the app, and the AR feature allows her to experiment with placement, scale, and combinations in her actual space, helping her visualize creative arrangements without needing to purchase. Her need is to gain inspiration and freely experiment with options. Her behavior includes testing multiple styles, combining pieces, and saving or sharing her favorite arrangements.

**Scenario 4: Emerging Artist**  
Sofía, 24, wants to showcase her artwork to a wider audience. She uploads images of her pieces to the app, and AR allows potential buyers to see how the artworks would look in real spaces. This helps buyers imagine the art in their own environments, increasing engagement and sales potential. Her need is to gain visibility, receive feedback, and generate income. Her behavior includes uploading artworks, displaying them in realistic contexts, and monitoring user interaction.

**Scenario 5: Professional Decorator or Interior Designer**  
Carlos, 38, is advising a client on decorating their living room. He uses the app to test different artworks and styles in the client’s space with AR, adjusting size, placement, and style. This allows him to present curated options and efficiently gather feedback. His need is to ensure that the chosen artworks harmonize with the overall design and satisfy the client. His behavior includes exploring multiple artworks, analyzing color and style combinations, and presenting visualizations for client approval.
