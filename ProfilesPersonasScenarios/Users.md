## Primary Users

- **Art Buyers:**  
People looking for unique pieces for their home, office, or studio. They need to make confident purchase decisions, considering style, size, and price, and ensuring the artwork fits their space and personal taste. Their behavior includes exploring options, reading about the artist and the piece, and choosing based on trust and personal preference.

- **Collectors:**  
Individuals interested in expanding their collection with emerging artists’ works or special pieces. They need to ensure authenticity, quality, and compatibility with their existing collection. Their behavior includes researching artists, evaluating different options, and making careful decisions based on detailed information about the artwork and its provenance.

- **Decoration Enthusiasts:**  
People seeking inspiration to decorate their personal spaces. They need ideas, combinations, and visual references that help them create harmonious and original environments. Their behavior includes exploring styles, mixing pieces, and saving or sharing ideas to plan their decoration effectively.

## Secondary Users

- **Emerging Artists:**  
Individuals aiming to showcase their work and generate sales. They need platforms that allow them to present their art clearly and attractively, receive feedback, and connect with potential buyers. Their behavior includes uploading pieces, describing them, and monitoring user engagement.

- **Decorators and Interior Designers:**  
Professionals who advise clients on art selection. They need tools that help them explore and present options, showing how artworks fit within their clients’ spaces. Their behavior includes analyzing styles, comparing pieces, and guiding clients to make informed purchase decisions efficiently.
