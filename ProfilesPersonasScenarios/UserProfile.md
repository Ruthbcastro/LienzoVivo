## User Profiles

**Home Art Buyer**  
Individuals aged 25 to 45, typically professionals, entrepreneurs, or interior designers, who seek to acquire artwork that complements their home or office decor. Their main motivation is to enhance the ambiance of their spaces and reflect their personal style through art. Their behavior includes exploring artworks online, comparing styles and sizes, researching artists and pieces, and making informed purchase decisions. They value a variety of artworks, clear pricing, and transparent purchasing options.

**Art Collector**  
Generally aged 35 to 60, these are established professionals, investors, or curators looking to expand their collection with unique pieces or works by emerging artists. Their motivation focuses on authenticity, quality, and cultural value. Their behavior includes researching artists and trends, attending exhibitions or auctions, and carefully evaluating potential acquisitions. They need detailed information about provenance, authenticity, and secure purchasing options.

**Decoration Enthusiast**  
Includes individuals aged 20 to 40, such as students, young professionals, or amateur designers, who seek inspiration to personalize and beautify their spaces. Their motivation is creativity and experimentation, combining styles and textures to achieve unique environments. Their behavior includes exploring trends online, sharing ideas on social media, and finding ways to harmoniously combine pieces. They need an intuitive interface, flexible customization options, and features to save and share combinations.

**Emerging Artist**  
Individuals aged 18 to 35, including art students, independent artists, or visual creators, who want to showcase and sell their work to a broader audience. Their motivation is gaining visibility, receiving feedback, and generating income through the sale of their pieces. Their behavior includes uploading their works to digital platforms, interacting with audiences, and experimenting with attractive presentation methods. They need tools to professionally showcase their art, customization options, and integrated sales channels or links to their online store.

**Professional Decorator / Interior Designer**  
Individuals aged 30 to 50, such as interior designers, architects, or decoration consultants, who select and recommend artwork for client projects. Their motivation is to ensure that the chosen pieces harmonize with the style and needs of the space. Their behavior includes evaluating multiple options, analyzing colors and styles, and presenting curated selections to clients for approval. They need precise visualization tools, a wide variety of artworks, and functionalities to present options and gather client feedback.
