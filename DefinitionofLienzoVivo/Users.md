## Identifying Primary and Secondary Users

To define the users, several analyses were conducted on art-related applications similar or related to the goal of Lienzo Vivo. Examples include Artsy, Smartist, Houzz, and Etsy. Houzz claims that its buyers benefit from being “the best ally if you are thinking about renovating or redecorating your home,” and The New York Times considers it one of the best apps for home decoration. Etsy, on the other hand, is described as a platform for finding gifts for coworkers, weddings, or anniversaries, as well as handcrafted jewelry and vintage items. Artsy presents itself as the largest art marketplace in the world, with over a million artworks available, primarily targeting collectors and buyers interested in acquiring art from recognized galleries or participating in online auctions, even offering the possibility to view pieces in augmented reality. Finally, Smartist focuses on artists and creators who want to showcase their work in realistic contexts through virtual interior mockups, allowing them to share their work on social media or sales platforms like Etsy, enhancing visibility and sales.

From this comparative analysis, it can be concluded that the studied applications serve different user profiles within the realms of art, decoration, and digital commerce: from collectors and investors interested in acquiring artworks (Artsy), to artists who need to showcase their creations attractively (Smartist), buyers looking for unique and personalized items (Etsy), and users seeking inspiration and solutions to decorate or remodel their homes (Houzz). These trends help identify and support the primary and secondary users of Lienzo Vivo, ensuring that users represent real motivations aligned with both the online art buying experience and the interest in augmented reality visualization applied to personal spaces.

### Primary Users
These are the main beneficiaries who will use the application regularly:  
- Art buyers looking to acquire unique pieces for their home, office, or workspace.  
- Collectors interested in exploring new artworks and emerging artists, with the possibility of visualizing pieces in their own space through augmented reality.  
- Decoration enthusiasts seeking inspiration and personalized solutions to integrate art into their spaces.  

### Secondary Users
These are users who also interact with the application, though indirectly or with complementary objectives:  
- Emerging artists and visual creators seeking to exhibit and sell their works to a wider audience.  
- Decorators and interior designers using the application as a support tool to recommend artworks to their clients.
