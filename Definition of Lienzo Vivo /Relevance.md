## Explain the Relevance and Usefulness of the Application

This application allows artists to connect with their buyers in a unique way, providing the buyer with an immersive purchasing experience that gives the illusion of touching the artwork they are about to purchase and seeing how it would look in their own home.  

This augmented reality experience not only makes the purchase more interactive, but it also helps reduce user uncertainty before acquiring a piece. By being able to visualize the artwork in their real space, buyers can evaluate size, color, and framing, which increases confidence in their purchase decisions and improves overall satisfaction.  

Additionally, the app adds value for both artists and collectors: artists gain visibility and can showcase their works more attractively, while buyers enjoy a personalized and technological experience that facilitates art selection. The combination of e-commerce and AR creates a closer connection between the user and the product, making the interaction clear, intuitive, and memorable.  

In terms of usability (user control and immediate feedback), the application allows users to move, rotate, and scale artworks within the scene, ensuring full control over visualization. This contributes to a more satisfying experience and reduces errors in perceiving the artwork's size and position.  

Furthermore, the relevance of this app lies in offering an innovative solution that efficiently integrates technology, art, and commerce, becoming a useful tool for those looking to acquire, display, or collect art safely and creatively.
