## User Behavior

Based on observations from similar apps, users typically:

- Actively explore multiple artworks before making a decision.  
- Interact with technology, using AR to assess size, color, and how the piece fits in their space.  
- Seek inspiration, even if they don’t intend to purchase immediately.  
- Make quick purchase decisions when the app provides clear feedback on availability and pricing.  
- Share and recommend artworks with friends, family, or design professionals.
