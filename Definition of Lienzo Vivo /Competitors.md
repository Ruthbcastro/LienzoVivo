## Competitors

### Artsy
Artsy positions itself as the world’s largest art marketplace, mainly targeting collectors and buyers interested in acquiring pieces from recognized galleries or participating in online auctions. It even allows augmented reality (AR) visualization of artworks. Its focus is on collectors and investors, offering a wide catalog and tools to explore and purchase art reliably.

### Smartist
Smartist focuses on artists and creators who want to showcase their work in realistic contexts using virtual interior mockups. This allows sharing pieces on social media or selling platforms like Etsy. However, users have reported issues such as cluttered scenes, interference from lamps or other objects, and lack of realism, highlighting the need for full scene control and clear feedback (user control and visibility principles).

### Etsy
Etsy provides a platform to find unique and personalized items, including artwork. It emphasizes ease of use and consistent presentation of information, adhering to principles of simple and intuitive navigation.

### Houzz
Houzz serves users looking to renovate or redecorate their homes. It allows users to visualize changes and gather inspiration for their spaces. The app focuses on clear, accessible information while maintaining consistency and usability.

### Artrooms
Artrooms allows users to visualize artwork in their own spaces using AR, with options to select frames and move artworks around. However, it faces stability issues, limited frame manipulation, subscription errors, and outdated content. These problems reduce user confidence, emphasizing the importance of consistency, user control, and visibility of elements.

### WallPicture
WallPicture is designed for placing and previewing art in the user’s real environment and facilitating purchases. Users report some advertised functions do not work, confusing pricing, and limited support. This affects perceived reliability and ease of use, highlighting the need for clarity, user control, and consistency.

### Art.com
Art.com offers a wide art catalog with AR visualization and direct purchase from the app. Users have mentioned instability in AR, limitations in frame customization, and issues loading large images, reflecting the need for stability, user control, and clear feedback.

### ART AR
ART AR targets artists, galleries, and stores, allowing artworks to be visualized in real spaces using AR. It presents challenges in surface detection, issues on older devices, and an initial learning curve for non-technical users, underscoring the importance of clear instructions, control, and feedback.
