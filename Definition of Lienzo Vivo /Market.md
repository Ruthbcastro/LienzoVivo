## Market

The Lienzo Vivo market consists of people interested in art, decoration, and unique shopping experiences. It includes:

- **Art Buyers:** Individuals looking for unique pieces for their home, office, personal spaces, or even as gifts.  
- **Collectors:** People seeking to discover new works and emerging artists.  
- **Decoration and Interior Design Enthusiasts:** Those looking for inspiration and practical solutions to integrate art into their spaces.  
- **Artists:** Creators aiming to showcase their work and reach an audience to sell their pieces.
