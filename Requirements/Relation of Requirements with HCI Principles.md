

# Relation of Requirements with HCI Principles

## 1. Visibility and Feedback

**RF1:** Display artwork info (name, artist, size, materials, price).
**Justification:** Users see all details immediately without memorization.

**RF4:** Show a visual indicator while waiting for wall alignment.
**Justification:** Keeps the user informed that the app is processing.

**RF5:** Change color of guide lines + show message “Borders correctly aligned.”
**Justification:** Provides clear confirmation of correct alignment.

**RF7:** Show progress bar during scanning.
**Justification:** Lets the user know how much is left to finish.

**RF8:** Show confirmation with “Scan completed.”
**Justification:** Users know the process ended successfully.

**RNF1:** Provide clear visual feedback in every stage.
**Justification:** Prevents uncertainty during the experience.

**RNF2:** Indicators must update immediately when the state changes.
**Justification:** Avoids a disconnect between user action and system response.

---

## 2. Consistency

**RF10:** Arrow, shaded border, and X button to reposition artwork.
**Justification:** Uses standard, familiar symbols that reduce learning curve.

**RF13:** Buttons “Done” and “Buy.”
**Justification:** Relies on familiar commands users already recognize.

**RF14:** Numbered walkthrough for each step.
**Justification:** Keeps guidance consistent throughout the experience.

**RNF5:** All messages, indicators, and visuals must be consistent.
**Justification:** Reinforces understanding and familiarity.

---

## 3. User Control and Freedom

**RF2:** User decides when to activate the camera for AR.
**Justification:** Respects user control over starting the experience.

**RF9:** User places the artwork by tapping on the screen.
**Justification:** Direct manipulation keeps placement under user control.

**RF11:** Touching the artwork activates drag mode.
**Justification:** Gives freedom to reposition within scanned space.

**RF12:** Artwork stays fixed once released.
**Justification:** Preserves the user’s intended action.

**RF10:** X button to cancel and reposition.
**Justification:** Supports undo without penalty.

---

## 4. Error Prevention

**RF3:** Guide lines and message to align the wall correctly.
**Justification:** Prevents errors in wall detection.

**RF5:** Color change and confirmation message.
**Justification:** Avoids confusion about whether alignment was correct.

**RF14:** Numbered walkthrough.
**Justification:** Reduces the chance of getting lost in the process.

**RNF3:** Intuitive interface.
**Justification:** A clear design minimizes user mistakes.

---

## 5. Recognition Rather Than Recall

**RF1:** Artwork info displayed directly on screen.
**Justification:** Users don’t need to remember details from memory.

**RF13:** “Done” and “Buy” buttons remain visible.
**Justification:** Familiar options that can be recognized, not recalled.

**RNF5:** Consistency in icons and messages.
**Justification:** Familiar visuals support recognition.

---

## 6. Minimal Cognitive Load

**RF6:** Clear instruction “Make circular movements” during scanning.
**Justification:** Simple guidance reduces mental effort.

**RF7:** Progress bar displayed.
**Justification:** Lowers anxiety about how long the scan will take.

**RF14:** Numbered walkthrough.
**Justification:** User doesn’t need to memorize the whole process.

**RNF3:** Intuitive interface.
**Justification:** Easier to understand actions step by step.

---

## 7. Compatibility and Flexibility

**RNF4:** Must work with supported devices and remain stable during AR scanning.
**Justification:** Ensures adaptability across devices and environments.
