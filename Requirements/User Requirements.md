
# Identify User Requirements

---

* **I want to be able to see if the wall is ready to place the artwork without having to guess or try multiple times.**
* **I want the icons, messages, and buttons to be clear and tell me exactly what to do at all times.**
* **I want the app to tell me step-by-step how to place the artwork, so I don't make mistakes or waste time.**
* **I want the buttons to always respond, without failures or delays, so I can buy or move the artwork without getting frustrated.**
* **I want to have all the artwork information available, so I know I am buying something authentic and of quality.**
* **I want the app to confirm that every action was completed correctly, so I don't doubt if something went wrong.**
* **I want to be able to browse many artworks without the app freezing or becoming slow.**
* **I want to see how the artworks would look in my space smoothly, without other objects or errors ruining the visualization.**
* **I want the messages and guides to be easy to understand, so I can try combinations and styles without getting frustrated.**
* **I want to easily save and share my favorite combinations, without losing my ideas.**
* **I want the app to help me quickly try combinations, without errors or confusion.**
* **I want to receive immediate feedback for every action, to work efficiently and reliably.**
