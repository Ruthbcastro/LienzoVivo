- **RNF1.** The application must provide clear visual feedback at all stages of scanning and artwork placement, ensuring **visibility of system status**.  
    _(Usability attribute: visibility and user awareness)_
    
- **RNF2.** Alignment, loading, and confirmation indicators must immediately reflect state changes in the prototype, ensuring **system responsiveness and efficiency**.  
    _(Usability attribute: responsiveness)_
    
- **RNF3.** The interface must be intuitive and guide the user step by step without external instructions, supporting **ease of learning** and **self-explanatory interaction**.  
    _(Usability attribute: learnability)_
    
- **RNF4.** The application must display consistent interactions throughout all steps of the workflow (scanning, placing, confirmation), maintaining **predictability** and **user control**.  
    _(Usability attribute: consistency and control)_
    
- **RNF5.** All messages, icons, colors, and visual elements must maintain consistency in style and meaning within the prototype, promoting **recognition rather than recall**.  
    _(Usability attribute: consistency and recognition)_
    
- **RNF6.** Simulated actions in the prototype must show immediate changes to reflect user feedback, reinforcing **user satisfaction** and **responsiveness**.  
    _(Usability attribute: feedback and satisfaction)_
    
- **RNF7.** The prototype must represent that the application does not store sensitive data, such as images, camera input, or location, ensuring **trust and perceived security**.  
    _(Usability attribute: trustworthiness and privacy)_
